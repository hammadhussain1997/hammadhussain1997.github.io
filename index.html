Common Questions

Before you reach out

Do you work with clients outside Pakistan?
Yes — most of my work is for clients in the US, UK, Australia, and UAE. All communication happens over email or WhatsApp text, so time zones aren't a blocker.
How do I pay you from another country?
I accept international payments through Payoneer and Wise — no local bank account needed on your end.
Fixed price or hourly?
Both. Most clients prefer a fixed price for a defined project, but I also work hourly or on a monthly retainer for ongoing work.
What if I need changes after launch?
Every project includes a revision window before launch, and I offer ongoing care plans for updates and fixes after that.
How long does a typical project take?
A landing page usually takes 3-5 days. A full business site or Shopify store typically takes 2-3 weeks depending on scope.
